# Remote Work Policy

Northwind Labs is a hybrid company. Employees may work remotely up to three days a week, and spend at least two days in the office each week.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Meetings should be scheduled inside these hours whenever possible.

Employees working remotely must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Public Wi-Fi may only be used together with the VPN.

Working from another country requires written approval from your manager and the People team at least 30 days in advance, and is limited to 20 working days per calendar year.
