# On-call Guide

Engineers join the on-call rotation after completing their first three months. Each shift lasts one week, starting Monday at 10:00.

When a page arrives, acknowledge it within 15 minutes. If you cannot resolve the incident within one hour, escalate to the secondary on-call engineer.

Severity 1 incidents affect all customers and need an incident channel and a status page update. Severity 2 incidents affect some customers.

After every severity 1 incident, the on-call engineer writes a blameless post-incident review within five working days.

On-call engineers receive one extra day of paid leave for every week on call.
