# Expense Policy

Submit expenses in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase date. Every expense needs an itemised receipt.

The home office allowance is 500 dollars per employee, available once every two years, for a chair, desk, monitor or similar equipment.

Meals while travelling for work are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

Book flights in economy class through the travel portal. Flights longer than eight hours may be booked in premium economy with manager approval.
